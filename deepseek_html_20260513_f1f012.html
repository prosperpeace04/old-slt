SLT Brochure

History: Department became full in 2006, carved out of Biology, Chemistry and Physics.

Career Prospects: Universities, Research Institutes, Quality Control, Pharmaceuticals, Agriculture.

Admission: 5 credits including Maths + two sciences.

Results Codes: NS, CO, CSO, WAR, SUS, WIT. Pass mark 40%.

SIWES: After ND1 Second Semester, 3-4 months.

Project/Seminar: Seminar = Ch 1-3 PPT; Project = full report Ch1-5.

    Project & Seminar Guide

    Seminar (SLT 212): Chapter 1-3 of your project topic as a slide presentation. Graded on clarity, research depth and presentation. No written exam.

    Project (SLT 222): Full report Chapters 1-5: Introduction, Literature Review, Methodology, Results, Discussion & Conclusion. Must include references. Defence required.

    Tips: Choose relevant topic, follow department format, use standard references.

    SIWES Resources

    Industrial Training after ND1: logbook templates, report writing structure, placement guidelines.

    Logbook: Daily activity summary, signature by supervisor. Report: Introduction, company profile, tasks, skills acquired, challenges, conclusion.

    Career Hub & NISLT Certification

    NISLT registration steps, CV templates for SLT graduates. Job roles: Lab Analyst, Quality Control Officer, Research Assistant, Water Treatment Tech, Microbiologist.

    Entrepreneurship Corner

    Reagent Preparation Service – Supply schools and labs with ready-to-use reagents. Low startup, high demand.
