SLT Brochure

History: Department became full in 2006, carved out of Biology, Chemistry and Physics departments.

Aim: Produce technicians capable of carrying out laboratory analysis and practical work under supervision of a technologist.

Career Prospects: Universities, Research Institutes, Quality Control in Breweries, Pharmaceuticals, Agriculture, Forestry.

Admission Requirements: 5 credits in WAEC/GCE/SSCE at not more than 2 sittings. Must include Mathematics + 2 from Physics, Chemistry, Biology. At least a pass in English Language.

Results System: NS = No Score (see lecturer), CO = Carry Over, CSO = Courses Still Outstanding, WAR = Warning, SUS = Suspension, WIT = Withdrawal. Pass mark = 40%. Minimum attendance = 70% per semester.

SIWES: Initiated 1973 by Industrial Training Fund. After ND1 Second Semester, lasts 3-4 months.

Project and Seminar: Seminar = Chapter 1-3 of project as slide presentation. Project = Full report Chapters 1-5.
